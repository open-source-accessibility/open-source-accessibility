# Sponsorship Opportunities

GitHub is proud to serve as the lead sponsor of the [Open Source Accessibility Summit (2026)](/2026%20Open%20Source%20Accessibility%20Summit/open-source-accessibility-summit-2026.md). We invite community members and organizations to join us in supporting this event through financial or in-kind sponsorships that help cover specific summit needs. Each sponsor will be recognized in event communications and onsite materials, with additional recognition based on the level of support.

If interested, please reach out to [Maria Lamardo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marialamardo/) for more details.

## Sponsorship Breakdown
| Sponsorship Opportunity | What the Sponsor Can Contribute | Estimated Cost | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AV & Technology Sponsor** | Provide funding, equipment, or technical support for microphones, sound, projection, recording, livestreaming, and room technology. | $5,000 | Gold |
| **Participant Housing Sponsor** | Provide or underwrite hotel room blocks in groups of 5 rooms for speakers, volunteers, or attendees who need lodging support. | $5,000 | Gold |
| **Room 1: Access & Inclusion Sponsor** | Underwrite ASL interpretation services for the full event or selected sessions. | $3,000 | Silver |
| **Open Source Accessibility Shirt Sponsor** | Provide or fund event shirts. | $3,000 | Silver |
| **Communication Access Sponsor** | Fund CART captioning services to ensure real-time access for attendees. | $2,000 | Bronze |
| **Room 2: Access & Inclusion Sponsor** | Underwrite ASL interpretation services for the full event or selected sessions. | $1,500 | Bronze |
| **Room 3: Access & Inclusion Sponsor** | Underwrite ASL interpretation services for the full event or selected sessions. | $1,500 | Bronze |
| **Room Sponsor** | Support the costs of summit rooms, including signage, supplies, technology, and participant materials. | $1,000 | Bronze |

Sponsors may combine contributions, such as funding a 5-room hotel block alongside CART captioning.

Sponsors should pay vendors directly, as GitHub cannot receive sponsorship funds directly for this event.

## Sponsor Recognition
Depending on the level of support, sponsors may receive:

### Gold Level
- Inclusion in post-event communications and community updates
- Inclusion of logo in the open source accessibility framework that will launch at the summit for 6 months
- Inclusion of logo in the open source accessibility framework that will launch at the summit for 3 months
- Opportunity to provide approved promotional materials or swag to attendees during the [Open Source Accessibility Summit](https://2026.allthingsopen.org/open-source-accessibility-summit)
- Recognition on the [Open Source Accessibility Summit](https://2026.allthingsopen.org/open-source-accessibility-summit) event site and [Open Source Accessibility Summit documentation](/2026%20Open%20Source%20Accessibility%20Summit/open-source-accessibility-summit-2026.md)
- Acknowledgment during opening and closing remarks
- Recognition as a sponsor of a specific service, room, or activity

- Acknowledgment during opening and closing remarks
